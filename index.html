Why infero

EU souvereign infrastructure
Your requests are processed in the EU datacenters by the EU company. Your data never leave the EU jurisdiction.
Zero data retention
All prompts and responses are erased once the session is finished. Nothing is stored, logged or used for training. All your data including code, documents and research stay yours. No intelectual-property leaks.
GDPR compliance
GDPR compliance is just easier when your data stays in the EU. We are not subject to the US CLOUD Act, and no foreign authority can compel us to hand over your data.
Confidentiality compliance
Client's information is erased with other data at the moment a new response is generated, facilitating the confidentiality requirements in regulated industries like law, tax and healthcare.
Fast inference
We run fast open-source language models on high-speed infrastructure and stream every token as it's generated, so answers start arriving right away.

Keep your OpenAI code.

Point any OpenAI SDK at our base URL, use your key and pick a model. Nothing else changes. 

import OpenAI from "openai";

const client = new OpenAI({
  baseURL: "https://api.infero.app/v1",
  apiKey: process.env.TT_API_KEY,
});

const res = await client.chat.completions.create({
  model: "glm-5.3",
  messages: [{ role: "user", content: "Hello" }],
});

console.log(res.choices[0].message.content);
import os
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    base_url="https://api.infero.app/v1",
    api_key=os.environ["TT_API_KEY"],
)

res = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="glm-5.3",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}],
)

print(res.choices[0].message.content)
